# Beyond work photos

Add your own photos directly to this folder. The homepage discovers `.jpg`,
`.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, and `.avif` files automatically when the site rebuilds;
uppercase extensions work too. Subfolders are ignored.
Convert iPhone HEIC/HEIF photos to JPEG before publishing; browsers do not
consistently support those formats and this gallery does not discover them.

- Use short, descriptive filenames such as `01-skiing-in-winter.jpg` or
  `02-electric-guitar.webp`. Photos appear in filename order, and hyphens or
  underscores become spaces in the image descriptions read by screen readers.
  Do not include private names, locations, or other sensitive information in filenames.
- Export web-sized copies (about 800–1200 pixels on the longer edge) and remove
  location/EXIF metadata before uploading. Keep the originals elsewhere.
- The gallery displays square frames. Crop photos to a square before uploading,
  removing any usernames or other personal details from Story screenshots.
  Nine or more photos produce three moving rows, with the middle row drifting
  in the opposite direction. Six to eight photos produce two rows; fewer photos
  produce one. An empty folder shows only the section introduction.
- Photos drift slowly, pause on hover, and zoom only slightly within their frames.
  Visitors can also use the pause button. Reduced-motion settings and browsers
  without JavaScript get static, horizontally scrollable photos.
- Photos have no links or lightbox and cannot be dragged from the gallery. Images
  on a public website are still publicly accessible; this is not copy protection.

This README is unpublished and is not included as a page in the built website.
